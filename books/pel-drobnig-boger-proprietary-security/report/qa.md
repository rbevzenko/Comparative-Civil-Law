A. General
These rules allow as an alternative method to the creation of a security right by granting
also the creation of such a limited proprietary right by retention. In the case of creation of a
security right by granting, the security provider grants a limited proprietary right to the
secured creditor, while there is in general no transfer of ownership. Where a security right
is created by retention, the secured creditor transfers ownership of the collateral, but re-
tains a security right. The transferee obtains only encumbered ownership even though the
secured creditor had held unencumbered ownership before the transfer.

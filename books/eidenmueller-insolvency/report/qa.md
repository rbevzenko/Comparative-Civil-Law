When should statutory insolvency proceedings with respect to a corporate debtor be opened? “The Initiation Problem in Bankruptcy”50 is certainly one of the most important insolvency policy questions that every jurisdiction has to answer in one way or another. Based on economic reasoning, the answer to this question seems straightforward: insolvency proceedings should be opened in case of financial failure of a company. More formally, the test is V = max(Vgc, Vl) < L, where V stands for the greater of the going concern value and the liquidation value of the company and L for its liabilities.

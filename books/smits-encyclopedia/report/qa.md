A discussion of legal reasoning can beneﬁt from a distinction between diﬀerent kinds of reasons. Contributive reasons are reasons which plead for or against a conclusion, but which do not determine by themselves whether this conclusion follows. The contributive reasons for a conclusion must be weighed against the contributive reasons against this conclusion, and whether the conclusion follows depends on the balance of all contributive reasons for and against it.

Duty of Secrecy
The relationship of banker and customer is of a confidential nature and, as a general rule, the banker is under
a duty of secrecy. The leading English case is Tournier v National Provincial Bank, where Atkin L.J.937
observed that this duty applies not only to information derived by the banker from the account, but extends
